Frequently Asked Questions

Should I form an LLC or a C-Corporation?

If you plan to raise venture capital or offer stock options, a C-Corp is best. If you want pass-through taxation and simplicity, an LLC is preferred.

Why is Delaware so popular for startups?

Delaware has highly developed corporate law, the Court of Chancery specifically for business disputes, and is universally preferred by investors.

Do I need an ITIN or SSN to form a company?

No, non-residents can form a US company and obtain an EIN without an ITIN or SSN.

What is a Registered Agent?

A person or entity authorized to receive legal documents and official government notices on behalf of your company.

Will I have to pay US taxes?

It depends on your entity type and whether you have 'Effectively Connected Income' (ECI) or a physical presence in the US. We provide detailed tax structuring advice.

How long does it take to get an EIN?

If you have an SSN, it is instant. For non-residents, it can take anywhere from 1 to 4 weeks depending on IRS processing times.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA)?

A treaty between two countries designed to prevent the same income from being taxed by both jurisdictions.

What is Transfer Pricing?

The rules governing the pricing of transactions between related enterprises under common ownership or control.

What does 'Arm's Length Principle' mean?

It mandates that transactions between related parties must be priced as if they were between independent entities in the open market.

How can I legally reduce withholding taxes on dividends?

By structuring your holding company in a jurisdiction that has a favorable DTAA network with the countries where your subsidiaries operate.

What is Base Erosion and Profit Shifting (BEPS)?

Tax avoidance strategies that exploit gaps in tax rules to artificially shift profits to low or no-tax locations. We ensure your strategies are BEPS-compliant.

Do you advise on Economic Substance Regulations (ESR)?

Yes, we ensure your entities meet ESR requirements in jurisdictions like the UAE and offshore centers.

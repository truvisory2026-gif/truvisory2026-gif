Frequently Asked Questions

What does ROC compliance entail in India?

It includes filing Annual Returns (AOC-4, MGT-7), maintaining statutory registers, conducting mandatory board meetings, and filing director KYC.

What are Economic Substance Regulations (ESR)?

Regulations requiring companies in certain jurisdictions (like the UAE) to demonstrate actual economic activity proportionate to their income.

What is Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) filing?

A mandatory declaration of the natural persons who ultimately own or control a company, required globally for AML compliance.

Do you provide Company Secretary services?

Yes, we act as the named Company Secretary for entities in Singapore, Hong Kong, and India where required by law.

What happens if I miss an annual filing deadline?

Penalties vary by jurisdiction but typically include daily accumulating late fees, striking off the company register, and potential director prosecution.

Can you assist with FEMA compliance in India?

Yes, we handle FLA returns, APR filings, and RBI compliance for inbound FDI and outbound ODI.

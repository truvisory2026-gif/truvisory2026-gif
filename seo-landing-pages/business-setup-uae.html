Frequently Asked Questions

Which UAE Emirate is best for business?

It depends on your sector. Dubai is ideal for general trading and tech, Abu Dhabi is excellent for government contracts and heavy industry, while Sharjah is a logistics and manufacturing hub.

Can I set up an Offshore company in the UAE?

Yes, jurisdictions like JAFZA and RAKICC offer robust offshore company structures ideal for wealth management and holding companies.

What taxes apply to UAE businesses?

The UAE introduced a 9% Corporate Tax on profits exceeding AED 375,000, and a 5% Value Added Tax (VAT).

Do I need to visit the UAE to set up my business?

Many Free Zone setups can be completed remotely. However, a visit is usually required for biometric visa stamping and final bank account opening.

How many visas can I get with my company?

Visa quotas depend on the size of your physical office and your specific business license. Free Zones usually offer a set number of visas based on the package selected.

What is the cost of setting up a business?

Costs vary significantly based on jurisdiction, license type, and visa requirements. We provide tailored, transparent quotes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the mandatory VAT registration threshold in the UAE?

Registration is mandatory if your taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 in the last 12 months or are expected to in the next 30 days.

Can I register for VAT voluntarily?

Yes, you can apply voluntarily if your taxable supplies/expenses exceed AED 187,500.

What is the standard rate of VAT?

The standard rate is 5%.

How often do I need to file VAT returns?

The FTA assigns a tax period to each registrant, which is usually quarterly, though some large businesses must file monthly.

What is the penalty for late VAT registration?

Failure to register on time incurs a flat penalty of AED 10,000.

Do Free Zone companies need to register for VAT?

Yes, unless they operate entirely outside the scope of VAT or within a 'Designated Zone' with specific criteria.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to travel to Hong Kong to set up a company?

No, the entire incorporation process can be completed remotely via e-Registry.

What is a Business Registration Certificate (BRC)?

It is a mandatory certificate issued by the Inland Revenue Department, required for all businesses operating in Hong Kong.

Is an audit mandatory in Hong Kong?

Yes, all Hong Kong incorporated companies must have their annual financial statements audited by a HK Certified Public Accountant (CPA).

What is the minimum share capital?

There is no minimum share capital requirement; usually, companies are set up with $10,000 HKD divided into 10,000 shares of $1 HKD each.

Can a foreigner own 100% of a Hong Kong company?

Absolutely, there are no restrictions on foreign ownership or nationality of directors.

Do you assist with opening a Hong Kong bank account?

Yes, though HK banks have strict KYC, we assist with documentation and introductions to our banking partners.

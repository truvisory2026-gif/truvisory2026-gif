Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Mainland and Free Zone?

Mainland companies can trade freely across the entire UAE market, while Free Zone companies are generally restricted to operating within the Free Zone or internationally, but offer 100% foreign ownership and specialized infrastructure.

How long does it take to register a company in Dubai?

Depending on the jurisdiction and activity, incorporation typically takes between 3 to 14 working days.

Do I need a local sponsor?

Recent changes allow 100% foreign ownership for many Mainland commercial and industrial activities. Most Free Zones have always allowed 100% foreign ownership.

What is the minimum share capital required?

Many Free Zones have no minimum share capital requirements, while others require a nominal amount. Mainland setups generally do not require depositing share capital upfront.

Can Truvisory help with resident visas?

Yes, we handle the entire visa process for investors, employees, and family dependents.

Is a physical office required?

Mainland companies typically require a physical office. Some Free Zones allow flexi-desk or virtual office options.

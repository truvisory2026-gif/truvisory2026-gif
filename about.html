Who We Are

Truvisory Financial Services Pvt. Ltd. is a business consulting and financial advisory firm built for founders who want more than compliance ticked off a checklist. We work alongside entrepreneurs, startups, SMEs and established companies to handle the financial and regulatory foundations of a business, so they can focus their energy on building it.

Our team brings together Chartered Accountants, tax specialists and business consultants under one roof. This means a client doesn't need to coordinate multiple vendors to launch, run and grow a company — Truvisory manages it as one integrated engagement.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To simplify business setup, compliance and international expansion through honest, expert advisory and dependable execution — so entrepreneurs can build with confidence.

Vision

To be the most trusted business advisory partner for entrepreneurs across India and the jurisdictions they choose to grow into.

Our Story

Truvisory Financial Services Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated in 2024, founded on a simple observation: most businesses don't fail from a lack of ambition, they stumble on the operational and regulatory decisions nobody explained to them clearly — the wrong entity structure, a missed compliance deadline, a jurisdiction chosen without proper due diligence.

The company was founded by Nivya Jain, who brings hands-on corporate marketing experience from her time with organisations including Thomas Cook and Citibank. That background in brand-building and customer-facing business strategy shapes how Truvisory approaches advisory today — with equal focus on financial compliance and commercial growth.

Backed by a team of Chartered Accountants, Nivya built Truvisory to combine strong financial and regulatory expertise with a practical, growth-oriented perspective. What began as a focused advisory practice has grown into a full-spectrum business consulting firm, supporting clients from their first incorporation filing to their entry into international markets such as the UAE, Singapore, Hong Kong, the UK and the USA.

The name itself reflects our founding principle — advisory should be true: transparent in its recommendations, practical in its execution and accountable for its outcomes.

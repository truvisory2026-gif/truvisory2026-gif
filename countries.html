India

Why India: The natural home base for most founders — a large domestic market, a maturing startup ecosystem and government incentives for new businesses.

Entity Types
  • Private Limited Company
  • Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)
  • One Person Company (OPC)
  • Partnership Firm / Sole Proprietorship
Documents Required
  • PAN & Aadhaar of directors/partners
  • Registered office address proof
  • Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)
  • Director Identification Number (DIN)

Timeline: Typically 7–15 working days for incorporation, subject to document readiness and government processing.

FAQs

Which entity type is right for my business?

It depends on your funding plans, liability preference and number of founders — our advisors will recommend the best fit during your consultation.

Do I need a physical office to register a company?

A registered office address is required, but it can be a residential or shared/virtual address in most cases.

UAE

Why UAE: Zero personal income tax, 100% foreign ownership in most sectors, and strategic access to Middle East, African and South Asian markets.

Entity Types
  • Mainland Company
  • Free Zone Company
  • Offshore Company
Documents Required
  • Passport copies of shareholders
  • Proof of address
  • Business activity and trade name approval
  • No Objection Certificate (if applicable)

Timeline: Free zone company setup can be completed in as little as 5–10 working days; mainland setup typically takes 2–4 weeks.

FAQs

Can I own 100% of my UAE company as a foreigner?

Yes, most Free Zone and many Mainland activities now permit full foreign ownership.

Do I need to be resident in the UAE to set up a company?

No, but a UAE residence visa is usually obtained as part of the setup process for owners who wish to operate locally.

Singapore

Why Singapore: A stable, pro-business economy with attractive corporate tax rates, strong IP protection and a strategic gateway to Southeast Asia.

Entity Types
  • Private Limited Company (Pte. Ltd.)
  • Branch Office
  • Representative Office
Documents Required
  • Passport/NRIC of shareholders and directors
  • Registered local address
  • Company constitution
  • Details of a locally resident director

Timeline: Incorporation can typically be completed within 1–3 working days once documentation is finalised.

FAQs

Do I need a local director to incorporate in Singapore?

Yes, at least one director must be a Singapore resident — we can assist with nominee director services if required.

What is the corporate tax rate in Singapore?

Singapore offers a competitive headline corporate tax rate with partial exemptions for new startups — our team can walk you through current rates during consultation.

United Kingdom

Why United Kingdom: A globally respected legal system, easy company formation and strong access to European and international markets.

Entity Types
  • Private Limited Company (Ltd)
  • Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)
  • Sole Trader
Documents Required
  • Passport/ID of directors and shareholders
  • UK registered office address
  • Memorandum & Articles of Association
  • Proof of address for directors

Timeline: Companies House registration can often be completed within 24–48 hours once documentation is ready.

FAQs

Can a non-UK resident register a UK company?

Yes, there is no requirement for directors or shareholders to be UK residents.

Do I need a UK bank account before incorporating?

No, incorporation can proceed first; we assist with bank account opening as a subsequent step.

Hong Kong

Why Hong Kong: A low, simple tax regime, no foreign exchange controls and a strategic gateway into mainland China and wider Asia.

Entity Types
  • Private Limited Company
  • Branch Office
  • Representative Office
Documents Required
  • Passport copies of shareholders and directors
  • Registered Hong Kong office address
  • Company Secretary appointment
  • Proof of address

Timeline: Incorporation is typically completed within 5–7 working days.

FAQs

Is there a minimum capital requirement?

No minimum paid-up capital is mandated for standard private limited companies.

Do I need to appoint a local company secretary?

Yes, Hong Kong law requires every company to appoint a company secretary who is a Hong Kong resident or licensed entity — we can provide this service.

USA

Why USA: Access to the world's largest consumer market, strong investor familiarity and flexible entity structures across all 50 states.

Entity Types
  • Limited Liability Company (LLC)
  • C-Corporation
  • S-Corporation (for eligible residents)
Documents Required
  • Passport of members/shareholders
  • Registered agent details
  • Employer Identification Number (EIN) application
  • Operating agreement / bylaws

Timeline: State-level registration can often be completed within 1–2 weeks, followed by EIN and bank account setup.

FAQs

Which state should I incorporate in?

It depends on your business model — Delaware and Wyoming are popular for their business-friendly laws, but the right choice depends on where you plan to operate.

Can I open a US company without visiting the US?

Yes, most of the incorporation and EIN process can be completed remotely; a US bank account may require additional steps.
